- [x] **M11: Deterministic Quality Assurance Veto Gate** (Completed: 2026-11-08)
  - [x] Zero circular trust inspection architecture (`backend/app/services/qa/`) across 12 distinct verification categories:
    1. `FACTUAL_ACCURACY` (15%): detects unverified or contradicted script claims and checks citation confidence scores.
    2. `VIDEO_TECHNICAL` (15%): inspects container streams, MP4 faststart atom, black frames (>0.25s critical veto), and freeze frames (>2.0s).
    3. `NARRATION_AUDIO` (10%): verifies EBU R128 loudness (-14 LUFS target), true peak clipping (ceiling -1.0 dBTP), and dead-air silence intervals (>1.5s).
    4. `VISUAL_ASSETS` (10%): scene asset coverage, minimum 1080p resolution, and consecutive identical asset repetition detection.
    5. `SCRIPT_QUALITY` (10%): spoken pacing cadence (~140 WPM), length boundaries, readability, and trigram repetition detection.
    6. `PACKAGE_COMPLETENESS` (10%): verifies completed MP4 render, approved thumbnail, and approved SEO metadata draft are present with zero orphaned links.
    7. `THUMBNAIL_QUALITY` (5%): checks exact 1280x720 (16:9) dimensions, file size strictly under 2.0 MB, and PNG/JPEG format compliance.
    8. `METADATA_SEO` (5%): title length (<=95 chars), chapters starting at mandatory 00:00, description depth, and search tags limit (<=500 chars).
    9. `COPYRIGHT_LICENSING` (5%): verifies commercial broadcast rights (`COMMERCIAL_USE_ALLOWED`, `CC0`, `AI_GENERATED`) and flags non-commercial/prohibited assets.
    10. `SECURITY_PRIVACY` (5%): high-entropy regex secret scanning across scripts, metadata, and tags for Google Gemini, OpenAI, Anthropic, ElevenLabs, GitHub, AWS, and JWT tokens (CRITICAL veto if exposed).
    11. `CAPTION_INTEGRITY` (5%): ASS/SRT syntax, timing monotonicity, and alignment against spoken narrative.
    12. `YOUTUBE_POLICY` (5%): enforces synthetic media disclosure flag (`syntheticMedia: true`) and scans for deceptive/scam signals.
